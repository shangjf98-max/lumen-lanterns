Lumen Lanterns

Bend the light. Wake every lantern.

Click a mirror to rotate it and steer the beam. Light all the lanterns before your rotations run out. Prisms split the beam; coloured glass tints it.

Mouse: click a mirror · Keyboard: arrows + Space, U undo, R restart, M mute

How to play

  • Goal: light every lantern on the board.
  • Mirrors: click to rotate. Each rotation costs one move.
  • Prisms: split the beam into two beams at right angles.
  • Coloured glass: tints the beam. Coloured lanterns need the matching tint.
  • Stars: finish at or under par for 3 stars. 6 levels in all.
